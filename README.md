## 🙋‍♀️ About Me

I’m an **Associate Technical Lead at WSO2**, leading the **Identity & Access Management (IAM) QA team**, with **8+ years of experience** in software testing, automation engineering, and quality assurance across enterprise-grade systems.

I combine hands-on technical expertise with strategic vision to design, scale, and maintain robust test automation frameworks that align with real-world product and organizational needs.

- 🏢 Currently at **WSO2** (IAM QA)
- 🌍 Based in **Sri Lanka**
- ✍️ I write on Medium: **Tech Learn Share** and **@niluka**
- 🤝 Happy to collaborate on **test automation / API testing / quality engineering**
- 📫 Reach me via LinkedIn / X (links below)

### Domains I’ve worked in
- **Identity & Access Management (IAM)**
- **Healthcare**
- **Stock Exchange systems**

### What I focus on
As a **Cypress Ambassador**, I specialize in modern automation tools such as **Cypress, Playwright, and Appium**, focusing on reliability, maintainability, and developer-friendly testing practices.

I also have strong experience in **API testing and automation** using **REST Assured** and **Postman**, ensuring end-to-end quality across web, mobile, and API layers.

I have extensive experience across the full **SDLC and STLC**, working in **Agile (Scrum)** environments and collaborating closely with engineering, product, and security teams to deliver robust and secure software.

Currently, I’m driving innovation in **AI-assisted test automation**, exploring **AI agents, MCP-style orchestration, and intelligent test optimization** to simplify workflows and improve efficiency.

I enjoy continuous learning and sharing knowledge through technical posts, diagrams, and practical examples—especially around **test automation, IAM testing, mobile automation, and quality engineering**.
